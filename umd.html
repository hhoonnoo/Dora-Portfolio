University of Maryland

Overview

During my time as a graphic design intern at the University of Maryland for the Stamp Student Union, I helped design ads, posters, flyers, and media graphics, and I was also able to promote diverse programs and events happening within the student times.

Tools

Timeline

Type

Internship

Role

Graphic Design Intern — Campaign Design, Design Consultation, Illustration

Asian, Pacific Islander & Desi American Heritage Month 2024 (APIDA)
"To the Table" is a campaign celebrating Asian, Pacific Islander, and Desi American Heritage Month 2024 at the University of Maryland. The Multicultural Inclusion Community and Advocacy (MICA) department chose the theme of "food gathering," emphasizing inclusion, welcome, vibrant chaos, and an abundance of food.
Client: Multicultural Inclusion Community and Advocacy (MICA) Deliverables: Print, Digital, Merchandise, Apparel
Study Buddies
"Study Buddies" is a University of Maryland campaign designed to promote the well-being of students by encouraging them to take regular breaks during finals. The campaign aims to reduce stress and enhance productivity by suggesting various relaxing and engaging activities during study sessions.
Client: Student Activities Deliverables: Print, Digital
Terps for Change Sticker Designs
Terps for Change (T4C) is a community service-learning program at the STAMP Student Union that partnered students with local nonprofits. They sought sticker designs to promote each focus area: Age & Ageism, Education, Environmental Conservation, Food Justice & Hunger, and Socioeconomic Inequity.
Client: Terps for Change Deliverables: Die-cut Stickers
Maryland Dairy Sticker
I designed a sticker for Maryland Dairy featuring Kermit the Frog, created by Jim Henson — a distinguished UMD alumnus. We aimed to capture summertime fun and pay tribute to Henson's legacy of bringing joy to people worldwide.
Client: Maryland Dairy Deliverables: Die-cut Stickers
